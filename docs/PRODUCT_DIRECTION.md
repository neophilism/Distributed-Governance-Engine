# Approved product direction — October 16, 2026

Distributed Governance Engine is the common governance/document platform. CivicQuill and CharterQuill are related branded hosted subscription products. Their primary delivery is hosted SaaS. Self-hosting, custom deployments and white-label branding are bespoke commercial arrangements; do not advertise them as standard plans or generate a separate engine fork.

The core owns reusable editor/governance/security mechanisms. CivicQuill owns civic/legislative workflows; CharterQuill owns corporate/member/shareholder workflows. Shared visual conventions, familiar controls, typography and a Quill family identity help users recognize the other product. Product catalogs and entitlement policies remain configurable.

Keep the three repositories and existing stable work IDs. DCE-* IDs and dce-c14n-v0-dev are compatibility identifiers from the initial foundation and are deliberately retained after the repository rename. LEG-* and CORP-* retain their original IDs as well. A product rename is not permission to change signed digest formats or invent completed work.
